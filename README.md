## 📌 Project Overview
BizCredIQ is a fintech-focused machine learning platform built to help evaluate business creditworthiness beyond traditional credit scoring systems.

Conventional lending models often fail to accurately assess emerging businesses due to limited formal credit history.

BizCredIQ addresses this by leveraging broader signals including:

- business operational maturity
- regulatory compliance indicators
- financial performance metrics
- transaction behavior
- market outlook
- collateral strength
- digital business trust indicators

The platform predicts a business credit score to support intelligent lending and risk assessment decisions.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Small and medium-sized businesses frequently face financing barriers because traditional credit scoring systems rely heavily on rigid historical credit records.

This creates exclusion for promising businesses with limited formal borrowing history.

BizCredIQ solves this by building an alternative AI-driven credit evaluation framework.
